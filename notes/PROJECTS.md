## Related explorations

Local review also found document-review and correspondence prototypes, email-assistance work, capture/mind-map tooling, and visual interaction experiments. They are not all ready to present as public products. Internal projects stay private; template copies and open-source derivatives are not counted as original standalone work.

The synthetic knowledge generator is distinct from a deployed institutional knowledge platform: generating connected documents does not establish retrieval quality, access-control enforcement or production adoption.

The public record separates source inspection, functional testing, model evaluation and independent user feedback. [Contribution evidence](CONTRIBUTIONS.md) · [How I work](HOW_I_WORK.md)
